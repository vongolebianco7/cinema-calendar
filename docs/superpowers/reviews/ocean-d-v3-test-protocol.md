# D-v3 final iPhone protocol

1. Open D, 100 films, seed cinemap-poc-01.
2. Wait 10 seconds; record HUD median FPS and long-frame count.
3. Observe motion for 30 seconds. Fail if visibly less smooth than D-v2.
4. Screenshot at 100 state. G1 PASS only if it reads as underwater ocean without explanation.
5. G2 PASS only if first impression is not polygon, primitive-CG, geometric demo, or clip-art assembly.
6. Switch 0/30/100/300/500 and confirm ecosystem growth without simple object-count clutter.
7. Keep 500 state open 10 minutes; fail on reload, blank, frozen animation or severe degradation.
8. Only after all gates pass may D-v3 be integrated into production Ocean.
